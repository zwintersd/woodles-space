about — woodles.space

woodles

.space — a personal monorepo

a little desktop of soft, made things — typefaces and letters, palettes and quiet timers. one repo, all the things, kept close.

open it like a drawer. everything inside was made by hand.

a few rooms are properly public now: the bestiary's gallery, the witch's shared world, letters marked to be read. that's the exhibit — what i've chosen to put on show. everything you make here is yours alone, saved in your own browser, unless you publish it the same way.
